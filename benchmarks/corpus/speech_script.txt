# The fixed speech script (docs/research/15, Phase 0). Read it aloud at a normal pace with
# natural pauses: about sixty seconds. The same text is rendered through every model with
# `vox bench target-f0` and is the WER reference of `vox bench quality` and `take --matrix`.
# No digits (word-error scoring would have to normalise them); plenty of sibilants, stops
# and soft phrase starts.

Please sit still for a moment and listen to the sound of this room.
Somewhere behind the wall a fan is turning, slow and steady, and the floor creaks when I shift my weight.
This is a test of the voice changer, so I will speak the way I usually do: sometimes quickly, sometimes with a pause.
Six thick thistle sticks. She sells seashells by the seashore, and the shells she sells are surely seashells.
Peter picked a peck of pickled peppers before the big black bus passed the bakery.
Now a quieter line, almost a whisper, to see whether the soft start of a sentence survives.
And now louder again! Can you hear me clearly? Does the voice still sound like the same person?
Outside, the rain kept tapping on the window while the kettle started to sing.
Thank you for listening. That is the end of the script.
